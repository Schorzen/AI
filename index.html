Bomb defusal, 10 rounds. You get a random side: Cross plants the C4, Counter defends and defuses.

Side: random each game. Cross plants the C4 (one player carries it): hold Plant inside site A or B for 3 s. Counter stops the plant, or holds Defuse next to the bomb for 5 s. Dead players drop their rifle, and the C4 if they carried it: walk up and tap Pick up. Swap switches pistol and rifle. Round 1 is pistols only.
Touch: left thumb moves, right side aims, orange button fires (drag it to aim), light aim assist. Best in landscape. Sign in to save your level and join the leaderboard.
Desktop: WASD, mouse, click to fire, right-click scope, R reload, Q swap, E plant / defuse / pick up, B buy, Space jump.
